## Duplicate and family assessment

There are 85 distinct problem statements among 250 records. Eighty-two statements occur more
than once, covering 247 records. This is expected: variants of one problem deliberately form
student-solution/error examples within one declared `problem_family`. No exact normalized
problem statement appears in more than one family, so the declared family split prevents this
direct leakage route. The retained concern is semantic near-duplication across differently
worded families, which has not received a mathematical-semantic similarity audit; researchers
should inspect new families before adding them to a frozen split.

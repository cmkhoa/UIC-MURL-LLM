# v0.1 prompt selection for held-out evaluation

## Decision

Freeze [`prompts/v0.1-few-shot.md`](../prompts/v0.1-few-shot.md) with
`Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct` at revision
`aa8e72537993ba99e69dfaafa59ed015b17504d1` for one held-out test evaluation.
Do not change the prompt, model revision, decoding settings, or evaluator between
this decision and that run.

The selection criterion is: maximize schema validity, then correctness accuracy,
then correctness macro F1. The two candidates tied on validity (45/48, 93.8%).
Few-shot then won correctness accuracy (51.1% versus 28.9%) and macro F1 (29.1%
versus 18.7%) on the frozen validation split. This is a choice between the two
evaluated prompts, not a claim that the chosen configuration is adequate for
autonomous grading.

The held-out test split was not read or evaluated for this decision.

## Qualitative review

Three of 48 outputs were invalid JSON because mathematical LaTeX used unescaped
backslashes: `int_sub_0035`, `int_trigsub_0111`, and `int_imp_0186`. The model
also over-predicted `correct`: among 45 valid outputs, it correctly recognized 17
correct examples but classified 10 `minor_error`, 9 `incorrect`, and 2
`insufficient_work` examples as `correct`. It exactly localized the first error
in 5 of 26 valid error-bearing examples.

These issues rule out autonomous use. They do not overturn the selection because
few-shot is the better of the two fixed candidates under the predeclared metric
ordering. The held-out run should report output validity separately and should
not be used to make another prompt-selection decision.

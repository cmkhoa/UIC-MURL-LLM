# v0.5 moderated taxonomy-balance validation experiment

## Design

v0.5 is a training-only sampling intervention motivated by v0.4's tradeoff.
It retains the v0.2 prompt and all frozen model, QLoRA, 60-step, decoding, and
validation settings. The training generator reads only `data/processed/train.jsonl`.

Each taxonomy has a minimum 25 training records; labels already above 25 are
not downsampled. Thus `none` remains at 68 records, while each of the other
nine labels has 25, for 293 records from all 155 source examples. This restores
`none` to 23.2% of records (68/293), compared with 10.0% in v0.4, and reduces
the maximum per-source repetition from 34 to 13. The number 25 is the largest
non-`none` training-label count, derived only from train counts.

| Sampling | `none` records | Total records | Maximum source repetitions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| v0.2 | 68 | 155 | 1 |
| v0.4 | 68 | 680 | 34 |
| v0.5 | 68 | 293 | 13 |

The fixed effective training budget consumes 480 records. It makes about 1.64
passes through v0.5, versus 3.10 through v0.2 and 0.71 through v0.4. This is a
consequence of holding 60 optimizer steps fixed and must inform interpretation.

The metadata records source, output, prompt, generator hashes and every
source-ID multiplicity. The frozen output SHA-256 is
`ea78f215770cf29880532e19126a9c27a28308bb1403c52152c7c995c1139826`.

## Results

The matching untouched base control produced 0 schema-valid outputs from 48
validation transcripts. The v0.5 adapter produced 47/48 valid outputs (97.9%).

| Version | Valid outputs | Correctness accuracy | Correctness macro F1 | Taxonomy macro F1 | First-error exact | First-error MAD |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| v0.1 | 48/48 | 58.3% | 43.7% | 31.7% | 51.7% | 0.48 |
| v0.2 | 47/48 | 66.0% | 49.3% | 20.7% | 53.6% | 0.42 |
| v0.3 | 47/48 | 72.3% | 53.0% | 19.7% | 50.0% | 0.41 |
| v0.4 | 46/48 | 58.7% | 45.5% | 32.7% | 71.4% | 0.32 |
| v0.5 | 47/48 | 63.8% | 47.9% | 32.0% | 53.6% | 0.36 |

v0.5 restores most of v0.2's correctness while retaining nearly all of the
taxonomy gain from v0.4. It does not beat v0.4 on taxonomy F1 or localization,
and it does not beat v0.3 on correctness. It is therefore the strongest
*balanced* candidate and should enter the pre-registered cross-family
comparison without evaluating the held-out test set.

The T4 run used the fixed Qwen revision and seed. Training completed 60 steps
over 293 sampled records (1.628 epochs; final training loss 0.4628). The three
locally verified result archives have SHA-256 values:

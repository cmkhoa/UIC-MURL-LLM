# v0.6 hierarchical multi-task SFT validation experiment

## Design

v0.6 tested the precommitted hierarchical multi-task loss schedule while
holding the v0.5 recipe fixed: Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct at revision
`aa8e72537993ba99e69dfaafa59ed015b17504d1`, seed `20261004`, NF4/FP16 QLoRA
(rank 8, alpha 16, dropout 0.05), 60 optimizer steps, greedy decoding, the
v0.2 system prompt, and the 293-record v0.5 training dataset. Its frozen
dataset SHA-256 was
`ea78f215770cf29880532e19126a9c27a28308bb1403c52152c7c995c1139826`.

Only the supervised loss schedule changed. For steps 1–30,
`overall_correctness` received 4× weight and both diagnostic fields received
1×. For steps 31–60, `first_error_step` and `error_taxonomy` received 4×
weight and correctness received 1×. All remaining assistant JSON tokens kept
1× weight. Training data were constructed solely from the training split; the
fixed 48-record validation split was used once for the untouched base control
and once for the reloaded adapter. The held-out test split was not read.

## Results

The matching untouched base control produced 0/48 schema-valid validation
outputs. The adapter produced 46/48 valid outputs (95.8%). Its 32 correctness
matches are 66.7% when format-invalid outputs count against all 48 examples.

| Version | Valid outputs | Correctness accuracy | Correctness macro F1 | Taxonomy macro F1 | First-error exact | First-error MAD |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| v0.1 | 48/48 | 58.3% | 43.7% | 31.7% | 51.7% | 0.48 |
| v0.2 | 47/48 | 66.0% | 49.3% | 20.7% | 53.6% | 0.42 |
| v0.3 | 47/48 | 72.3% | 53.0% | 19.7% | 50.0% | 0.41 |
| v0.4 | 46/48 | 58.7% | 45.5% | 32.7% | 71.4% | 0.32 |
| v0.5 | 47/48 | 63.8% | 47.9% | 32.0% | 53.6% | 0.36 |
| v0.6 | 46/48 | 69.6% | 50.0% | 24.5% | 63.0% | 0.33 |

The precommitted utility uses correctness over all 48 examples, taxonomy F1
over valid outputs, and first-error exact match over 27 eligible valid
outputs. v0.6 scores `(32/48 + 0.2449 + 17/27) / 3 = 0.5137`, compared with
v0.5's `(30/48 + 0.3201 + 15/28) / 3 = 0.4936`. Thus the staged loss improved
correctness and first-error localization, but reduced taxonomy discrimination
by 7.5 points and produced one fewer valid output.

## Decision

v0.6 does not meet its prospective gate of at least 47/48 valid outputs, even
though its utility is higher. It therefore does not replace v0.5 as the frozen
Qwen recipe for the controlled cross-family comparison. The result supports
the causal hypothesis that late diagnostic weighting improves localization,
but it does not provide a sufficiently robust all-task candidate on this
small validation set. Do not evaluate either recipe on the held-out test set
yet.

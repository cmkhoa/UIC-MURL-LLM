# v0.7 two-stage diagnosis validation experiment

## Design

v0.7 tested whether v0.5's strongest balanced recipe could gain the useful
late-diagnosis behavior seen in v0.6 without losing eligibility. Stage 1 trained
the frozen v0.5 data and ordinary assistant-only SFT objective for 30 optimizer
steps. Stage 2 loaded the saved Stage 1 checkpoint and continued for 30 steps,
weighting the complete `first_error_step` and `error_taxonomy` fields 2× while
keeping `overall_correctness` and all remaining assistant JSON tokens at 1×.

The run held fixed Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct revision
`aa8e72537993ba99e69dfaafa59ed015b17504d1`, seed `20261004`, NF4/FP16 QLoRA
(rank 8, alpha 16, dropout 0.05), the 293-record v0.5 training dataset, the
v0.2 guidance prompt, the 60-step schedule, and greedy decoding. The training
dataset SHA-256 was
`ea78f215770cf29880532e19126a9c27a28308bb1403c52152c7c995c1139826`.
Training used only the frozen training-derived data. The held-out test split was
not read, tokenized, or evaluated.

The Stage 1 checkpoint was saved at step 30. A fresh model/trainer instance
then resumed from that checkpoint at step 31 with optimizer, scheduler, RNG,
and trainer state restored. The learning-rate schedule remained the single
precommitted 60-step schedule and did not restart at the stage boundary.

## Results

The matching untouched base control produced 0/48 schema-valid validation
outputs. The final v0.7 adapter produced 48/48 valid outputs. Because all
outputs were valid, its 31 correctness matches are both 64.6% valid-only
accuracy and 31/48 (64.6%) accuracy over the full validation split.

| Version | Valid outputs | Correctness accuracy | Correctness macro F1 | Taxonomy macro F1 | First-error exact | First-error MAD |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| v0.5 | 47/48 | 63.8% | 47.9% | 32.0% | 53.6% | 0.36 |
| v0.6 | 46/48 | 69.6% | 50.0% | 24.5% | 63.0% | 0.33 |
| v0.7 | 48/48 | 64.6% | 48.3% | 30.3% | 55.2% | 0.35 |

v0.7's first-error result is 16/29 eligible valid outputs. By difficulty,
correctness was 16/26 on hard examples and 15/22 on medium examples; taxonomy
macro F1 was 23.2% and 31.4%, respectively. Trig integrals remained the weakest
correctness topic at 2/7, while integration by parts and substitution each
reached 5/6.

## Precommitted decision

The precommitted balanced utility is:

```
utility = (correctness_accuracy_all_48
           + taxonomy_macro_f1_valid_only
           + first_error_exact_match_valid_only) / 3
```

v0.7 scores `(31/48 + 0.302944 + 16/29) / 3 = 0.5002`, compared with v0.5's
`0.4936`. It passes four of the five replacement criteria:

| Criterion | Required | v0.7 | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Schema-valid outputs | at least 47/48 | 48/48 | Pass |
| Correctness over all 48 | at least 30/48 | 31/48 | Pass |
| Taxonomy macro F1 | at least 0.3201 | 0.3029 | **Fail** |
| First-error exact | at least 53.6% | 55.2% | Pass |
| Balanced utility | strictly above 0.4936 | 0.5002 | Pass |

The taxonomy floor is binding. Under the precommitted all-criteria rule, v0.7
does not replace v0.5. Retain v0.5 as the frozen Qwen recipe for controlled
cross-family comparison. The result is nevertheless informative: moderating
v0.6's late diagnostic weight restored full validity and most of v0.5's
taxonomy performance while slightly improving correctness and localization,
but it did not preserve the required taxonomy floor.
